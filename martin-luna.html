The challenge

  • Heritage learners understand more Spanish than they speak. Most “bilingual” kids' apps are flashcards with cartoons — they drill words but never build the desire to read.
  • The grown-up who reads with the child is usually the one who decides what gets used — and most products ignore them completely.

The design

  • Story first, not drills: the app opens on the story itself. Activities and words unlock from the narrative — the child reads because they want to know what happens.
  • Two languages, one tap: an EN/ES toggle on every screen. The child controls the scaffold — never stuck, never babied.
  • A voice children want to imitate: warm narration in both languages, with speed control for accessibility.
  • Grown-ups included: a reading companion with before/while/after guidance, objectives, and answer keys — designed for the person who actually chooses the product.

What's inside

Production details

  • HTML5 web app — runs in any browser, no install, no login. Built for the devices families actually own.
  • Voice narration in English and Spanish on every story page, with Listen and Speed controls for accessibility.
  • Full EN/ES toggle across story, activities, words, and grown-ups sections — one codebase, two languages.
  • Companion to the Martín & Luna picture-book series (10 books) — the same characters children already meet on paper, now interactive.
