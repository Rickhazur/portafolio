Beyond Words: English for Latino Professionals — flagship case study

The problem

Spanish-speaking professionals plateau at intermediate English. They translate from Spanish word by word, hesitate in meetings, and sound less competent than they are. Traditional courses teach grammar rules; nobody teaches them to sound natural under pressure.

The analysis

  • Audience: adult professionals, B1 moving toward C1, mostly Latin American.
  • Root cause: lack of automatized lexical chunks and discourse frames — the prefabricated language fluent speakers rely on.
  • Contexts that matter: meetings, emails, negotiations, presentations, feedback.

The design

A 5-module curriculum built on a simple progression: Know → Choose → Produce. Module 1 teaches 18 lexical chunks (what they mean), Module 2 trains selection in professional situations (when to use them), Module 3 builds production with 10 discourse frames (how to sound fluent). Advanced grammar and pronunciation follow the same transfer-first logic.

The build

  • Authored in Rise 360 with a consistent visual system (teal/amber brand, light professional style).
  • 15 custom HTML5 games and activities — chunk sorters, conversation builders, email fixers, branching scenarios.
  • AI-cloned voice narration synced with caption bars, so audio, text, and visuals land together.
  • A branching capstone: learners run a high-stakes presentation where their choices change the outcome.
Result: a complete learning ecosystem — not a slide deck. Quizzes are built for transfer (new professional situations, not recall), and every activity mirrors a real workplace moment. Pilot in progress.
