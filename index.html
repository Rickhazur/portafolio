JUDGMENT ✕

“You’re careless”
is a verdict.

Maria can only defend herself — or shut down.

DESCRIPTION ✓

Describe the behavior,
not the person.

Facts open a conversation.
Verdicts close it.

STEP 01

Open with intent

Ask permission first: “Maria, can I share something I’ve noticed? I want this project — and you — to succeed.”
Permission turns a threat into a conversation.

STEP 02

Describe — don’t label

Use S-B-I: facts, not verdicts.

S
Situation
“In yesterday’s client meeting…”
B
Behavior
“The report went out without the updated figures.”
I
Impact
“We lost a day rebuilding trust.”
STEP 03

Build forward together

Ask: “What’s your take? What would help next time?”
Then agree on ONE concrete step.
Feedback that ends in the past is blame. Feedback that ends in the future is coaching.
