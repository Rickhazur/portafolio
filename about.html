About Me

Teacher for 22+ years. Translator for even longer. Now I build the courses I always wished existed.

I'm Ricardo Torres, an instructional designer based in Bogotá, Colombia. For over two decades I've taught English in Bogotá and Chicago — and since 1997 I've worked as a translator and interpreter between English and Spanish.

That combination is rare, and it's my edge: I don't just design learning experiences — I design them bilingual from the ground up, so Latin American learners never get a second-class translation of someone else's course.

  • 22+ years teaching in Colombia and the USA
  • Translator / interpreter EN–ES since 1997
  • TEFL certified — University of Toronto
  • Google Certified Educator (Level 1 & 2)
  • Based in Bogotá, Colombia · working worldwide
