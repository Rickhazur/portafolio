Feedback That Works

A 2-minute video microlearning course for managers: how to deliver difficult feedback without damaging trust. Scripted, storyboarded, produced, and narrated end to end.

The challenge

  • Managers avoid difficult conversations — or handle them so badly that trust collapses.
  • Traditional training is slide decks about feedback models nobody remembers.
  • The learning had to fit a busy manager's day: under 2 minutes, one clear framework.

The design

  • Scenario-first: open on the conflict, then rewind and rebuild it the right way.
  • Color-coded pedagogy: red = the wrong way, teal = the right way — the learner feels the difference before it's explained.
  • One framework, three steps: open with intent · describe with S-B-I, don't label · build forward together.

Production details

  • HyperFrames — open-source HTML-to-MP4 video framework: the entire video is a web-native composition (HTML/CSS/JS + GSAP animation), rendered to 1080p MP4. Every frame is editable and reusable.
  • ElevenLabs — AI voice generation: a cloned narrator voice, a directed male voice for David, and a directed female voice for Maria. Full dialogue scene with chat-bubble treatment.
  • AI-generated stills — character-consistent cinematic images with Ken Burns camera motion.
  • Karaoke-style closed captions — every word highlighted in sync with the narration, for accessibility and retention.
  • Kinetic typography synced to the voiceover; color-coded pedagogy (red = wrong way, teal = right way).
